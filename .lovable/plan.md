

# Royal East Wing — 5-Star Luxury Hotel Website

## Branding & Design System
- **Color palette**: Gold (#C6A75E), White, Soft Ivory/Champagne, Deep Charcoal for contrast
- **Typography**: Playfair Display for headings, Poppins for body text (via Google Fonts)
- **Design language**: Generous whitespace, soft shadows, gold hover accents, glassmorphism sections, smooth scroll animations (fade-in, slide-up), parallax hero effects

## Navigation
- Sticky transparent navbar that transitions to solid white on scroll
- Gold logo/wordmark "Royal East Wing"
- Links: Home, About, Rooms & Suites, Dining, Spa & Wellness, Events, Contact
- Mobile hamburger menu with elegant slide-in drawer

## Pages

### 1. Home Page
- **Hero**: Full-screen background image with "Where Luxury Meets Majesty" heading, subtitle, and two gold CTA buttons (Book Your Stay, Explore Suites) with parallax scroll
- **About Preview**: Split layout (image + text) highlighting 5-star service, location, and awards with fade-in animation
- **Luxury Experiences**: 4 elegant cards — Presidential Suites, Fine Dining, Spa & Wellness, Events & Conferences — with gold border hover and lift effects
- **Gallery Preview**: Image grid with subtle zoom-on-hover
- **Testimonials**: Review cards with gold star ratings
- **CTA Banner**: Full-width gold gradient with "Indulge in the Royal Experience" and Reserve Now button

### 2. About Page
- Hotel story & legacy section
- Vision & service philosophy
- Leadership team showcase
- Awards & recognition
- Gold line section dividers throughout

### 3. Rooms & Suites Page
- Four room categories: Deluxe Room, Executive Suite, Presidential Suite, Royal Penthouse
- Each with image placeholder, description, amenities list, price placeholder, and Book Now button
- Luxury card layout with scroll-reveal animations

### 4. Dining Page
- Sections for Signature Restaurant, Private Dining, Wine Lounge, Rooftop Bar
- Dark + gold aesthetic
- Menu preview placeholders

### 5. Spa & Wellness Page
- Massage therapies, Sauna & steam, Infinity pool, Fitness center
- Calm white + gold palette with soft animations

### 6. Events & Conferences Page
- Ballroom, Corporate conference rooms, Wedding hosting, Private celebrations
- Event gallery placeholder section

### 7. Contact & Reservations Page
- Contact info (location, phone, email placeholders)
- Embedded Google Map placeholder
- Reservation form with: Name, Email, Check-in/out dates, Room type select, Message
- WhatsApp booking button

## Shared Components
- **Footer**: Gold accents, social media icons, quick links, contact info
- **Scroll animations**: Fade-in and slide-up on scroll throughout all pages
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first design, all pages fully responsive

## Technical Notes
- All frontend only (no backend needed initially) — placeholder images and content
- React Router for multi-page navigation
- Google Fonts for Playfair Display + Poppins
- Intersection Observer for scroll animations
- Future-ready structure for booking system, payments, and loyalty program integration
